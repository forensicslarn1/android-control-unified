A captured frame is treated as an evidence item with its own record—not merely a visual export.

The operator initiates capture from the active mirror frame. The application does not save screenshots in the background.

HASHED

Add to case bundle

The image and Markdown/JSON record join a user-initiated local export alongside other selected evidence.

UX PRINCIPLE

Make the evidence context visible at the moment of capture. The user should know what will be saved, how it is labeled, and which case record it belongs to before export.
