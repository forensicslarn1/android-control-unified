07

The browser-local, non-rooted Android workflow should not claim raw packet capture. Instead, it should preserve an already authorized PCAP, PCAPNG, or diagnostic artifact as a documented evidence intake.

PRIVACY CONTROL

Packet captures can contain identifiers, credentials, or content. The interface must make that risk, bundle size, and source provenance visible before export—not after it leaves the browser.
